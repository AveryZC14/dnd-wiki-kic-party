A thick, slimy, purple tentacle with multiple eyes embedded across it. The shoulder is this big kind of octopus head with one eye, the tentacle is quite long and itself has 3 eyes.

It's quite uncomfortable as it's constantly pretty slimy, and requires stella to hydrate more than usual. 


This allows stella to use one handed weapons.

Tentacle whip:

Transmutation Cantrip (Stella)

Casting Time: Action

Range: 30 feet

Components: V, S

Duration: Instantaneous

Your tentacle lashes out at your command toward a creature in range. Make a melee attack against the target. On a hit, the target takes 1d6 Piercing damage, and if it is Large or smaller, you can pull it up to 10 feet closer to you.

Cantrip Upgrade. The damage increases by 1d6 when you reach levels 5 (2d6), 11 (3d6), and 17 (4d6).
