# Appearance
Dark skin, blue eyes. Short, greying hair, and scars all over. Quite muscled, wears a dark blue undershirt with studded leather armour. Has multiple bangles, necklaces, and other cool accessories that are magic items. Has multiple monster pelts sewn into his clothing.

# Traits
Oldest child of the Numaith family. A very powerful adventurer

# Personality
- Friendly, Approachable, A good teacher.

# Key Info
- Works as a freelance high-level adventurer.
	- Often does jobs for militaristic noble houses, such as [[House Kjellsvard]] and [[House Vocalle]]

# History
<kp-start></kp-start>
Some time ago, took down the leader of the [[Cult of Lathander]]. From them, took the [[Enchanted Maul]].

While working a few jobs for [[House Kjellsvard]], Was tasked with briefly training [[Thorin Kjellsvard]]. During this training, he realises that Thorin is not cut out for the solider life, and presents him with the [[Enchanted Maul]], with the promise that Thorin will do good with this gift. Also explains to him the way to break out.
