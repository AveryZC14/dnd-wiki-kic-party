Our unnamed adventuring party meet again at their tavern, save for one sleepy [[Talia Waterbranch|Talia]]. They've only been on one adventure before, but that's a tale for another time. Axengrav asks a favour, to help poor [[Dennis Clorvian]], who fled his home after his dangersense ring glowed red, spotting a terrifying murderous rat on his way out. Dennis is the adopted son of [[Cassius Clorvian]], the inventor of the incredibly effective product Clorvie Floor Cleaner! He's also currently away on a work trip. 
After forgetting which room Talia lives in, The party go north to the Clorvian household, seeing a troupe of goblins ransacking the place! After thoroughly beating up the goblins [[Terry, Derry, Kerry and Frank]] and Burning Derry practically to death, They give the goblins small trinkets so that they will leave. In the basement, They find crates of potion ingredients and a group of rats. They pacified the rats with cheese and [[Ronda Dimplethatch|Ronda]] talked to them, learning that a dangerous room lay ahead. Up ahead is a puzzle, which the party solved by cross referencing the crates and a document from a locked chest in Cassius' room. In the lab, they encounter a ferocious giant spider and a deadly inferno spider, which they kill with the help of a big vat of acid. Turns out, the earthquake the previous night tipped some potions over, which the rats and spiders drank to become huge and deadly. The party returns to [[Scaramouth]], and they're promised compensation from Cassius whenever he returns from his work trip.
