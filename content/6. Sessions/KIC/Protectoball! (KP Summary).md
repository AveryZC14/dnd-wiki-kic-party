A mysterious and seemingly armless adventurer by the name of [[Stella]] enters the [[scaramouth]], asking to join an adventuring party, as their old one has disbanded. Our unnamed adventuring party, save for one sleepy [[Talia Waterbranch|Talia]], returns from the most boring commission ever, helping a Tiefling get his cat unstuck from a tree or something. The party agrees to let Stella on a trial mission together and plans to meet up at 7:30 the next morning to get the jump on the new commissions for the day. That night, Ronda gets a letter from [[Merla Solanum]], her protegee, saying that she's discovered a new recipe for the thatchery, fries! potatoes can do so much. and they have to, since [[Fieldybrooke|Ronda's hometown]] pretty much only has potatoes.
The next morning, the party gathers to find that every single commission is too hard for them :(. All of a sudden, [[Dewey Shirelon]] busts in, pleading someone to help him get his kidnapped son back. An elven duo, ([[Jacques Mclauren|Jacques]] and [[Jacquelynne Mclauren]] of [[House Mclauren]]) attempt to scam the poor man into generational debt, and are interrupted by our party. Notably, despite wearing decently fancy noble clothing, [[Filarion Phrygia|Filarion]] doesn't reveal his nobility to the sussy mclaurens. Eventually They're persuaded to leave and the party agrees to help mr Shirelon At the [[Dewey and Linda's Smiths and more|Shirelon Household]], it seems like a brawl has broken out inside the shed, where the blacksmithing and artificing happens. Surprisingly, nothing was stolen. As Thorin looks upon a massive shield, he gets a flashback of the day he told his parents he wanted to leave his role as the future leader of [[House kjellsvard]]'s wars, and wanted to be an adventuring rogue. His parents were displeased. The Shirelons tell the party of the "protectoball", a contraption to keep their son Billy safe, and lend the party a magical chisel used for breaking metal. Outside the shirelon house, there are massive claw marks heading south, so the party follows.
along the way, they encounter an encampment of goblinoids, which they'll have to pass to cross the river. After a brief fight with the bugbear outcasts Charglar and Klax, knocking out their hobgoblin leader Idris, they find the [[Terry, Derry, Kerry and Frank||4 goblins from last session]], and make peace. The goblinoids talk of their rejection from goblin society, and goblinoid discrimination throughout other humanoid societies. The adventuring party continues south. Our party approaches the ruins of a town, and a stone structure probably once used for barracks. They find two strange draconic creatures, one young white dragon that seemingly has patches of brown feathers, and a drake with strangely metallic scales. These creatures are chained together, and it seems the protectoball closed itself around the chains. After a ruthless battle, [[Ronda Dimplethatch|Ronda]] sticking the dragon in the face with an arrow, Thorin failing repeatedly to hurt the drake and stella somehow using just their feet to deal the killing blow on the drake, Billy is saved. The dragon's organs are found to be strangely doubled, and its flesh rotten and diseased. Also, the party takes the scales off the drake to bring to [[Linda Shirelon]]. Finally, they decide to accept stella as the 5th member of their adventuring party. Another W in the bag!
