Blond hair that's always in a plait, blue eyes, halfling height. Wears the same flannelette shirt over all her outfits.

# Backstory
left her sleepy home town of [[Fieldybrooke]], where she ran the much loved restaurant[[ The Thatchery]], to explore exotic cuisines and bring back ingredients for her own kitchen, particularly the kind of ingredients you can only get by adventuring (monster guts)! The special ingredient in all her recipes is a prayer to the goddess of agriculture.
