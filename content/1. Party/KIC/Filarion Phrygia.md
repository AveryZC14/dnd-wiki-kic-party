# Appearance
Long, straight, platinum blond hair. Green eyes. Often wears expensive looking clothes

# Backstory
From the elven noble family of [[House Phrygia]], he was raised mainly by tutors and family servants. In particular, his magic tutor [[Caelynn]] taught him his magic, and gave him a quarterstaff before he was sent off to adventure by his family. Due to the (relatively) low status of the Phrygias, he is eager to prove himself and bring honour to the family name
